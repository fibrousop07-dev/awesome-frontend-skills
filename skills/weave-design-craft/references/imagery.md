# Imagery and evidence
Judge an image by opening it, not by its filename, URL or a successful HTTP response. Confirm subject, composition, crop and tonal fit. Never relabel unrelated stock as a specific ingredient, client, place or product. Record the particular source and permitted use; disclose representative or generated imagery. Finish the representative screen with real licensed assets or purposeful original art before visual review. Missing images and placeholder slabs block delivery unless the user requested a wireframe. Resize and compress assets, preserve layout dimensions, and test mobile crops and failed loads. Keep alt text accurate and concise.
