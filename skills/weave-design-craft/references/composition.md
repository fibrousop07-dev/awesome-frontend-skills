# Composition and brand
Build one representative, fully populated screen before expanding the site. Decide what the visitor should notice, understand and do. Choose a composition that expresses this business; a row of alternating text/image sections is not an art direction. Explain the relationship between type, image crops, spacing, materials and interaction in a compact DESIGN.md. Reuse an existing brand system where supplied.
Vary density and proportion when the content warrants it. Prefer specific content and one memorable, useful design choice to decorative filler. A sample or reference is evidence to study, not a universal template or permission to copy a brand. Preview at desktop and mobile sizes, then revise the actual artifact rather than writing another design essay.
