# Interaction and state coverage
List states only for the controls actually present: default, invalid, busy, success, empty or unavailable as appropriate. Test the main task end to end, not just the first click. Forms must enforce their real ranges and business rules, associate errors with fields, and focus the next actionable location. Simulated submission must say that nothing was sent. Navigation and dialogs need keyboard access, Escape, sensible focus movement and restoration. Content remains usable without animation and, where practical, without JavaScript. Reuse accessible primitives rather than guessing that appearance implies correct behavior.
